For the person running the study. Keep them closed while raters use this device.

Item numbers and labels follow the form version chosen on the Report tab. The default is the WHO daily reporting form marked Ver 2019 WHO. Injury definitions for that version are quoted word for word from its WHO definitions sheet, as printed. The code was written by an AI assistant. The study view is modelled on Jafar, Sergeant and Lecky, Emerg Med J 2020;37:58 (clinicians code injury vignettes, agreement by Randolph's free-marginal kappa) and adds a tool arm. The vignettes here are placeholders written for this prototype and are not the published ones.
